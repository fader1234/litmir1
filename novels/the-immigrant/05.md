The next morning, Charles is very nervous. He must do something. He remembers the kind woman from his first day. He does not know her name, but he remembers her face. He goes to the same street. He hopes to see her.

He waits for one hour. Then he sees her! She is walking to a shop.

Charles walks to her. "Hello," he says. "Do you remember me?"

The woman looks at him. She smiles. "Yes, I remember you. You are Charles. How are you?"

"I need help," Charles says. He is afraid, but he tells her the story. He tells her about the diamond, the news, and the police.

The woman listens. Her name is Anna. She is a teacher. She is a good person.

"This is a big problem," Anna says. "But you are a good man. I want to help you."

Anna has an idea. She knows the director of the museum. She can talk to him.

They go to the museum together. Anna talks to the director. Charles waits. He is scared.

Then the director comes out. He looks at Charles. "Thank you for returning the diamond," he says. "You are an honest man."

The director is happy. He gives Charles a reward. It is not a lot of money, but it is enough for a new start.

Charles is very happy. He thanks Anna and the director. Now he has money for a good room and food. He feels hopeful.

"Anna," Charles says. "Thank you for everything. Please, can I invite you to dinner? To say thank you."

Anna smiles. "Yes, Charles. I would like that."

That evening, they go to a small restaurant. They eat and talk.

**Anna:** So, Charles, why did you come to Sinea? You are a good man. You left your home. There must be a big reason.

**Charles:** *(He looks down.)* It is... a new start. For a job.

**Anna:** *(She looks at him. She is not sure.)* A new start? But you have no papers. It is very dangerous. It is a big risk for only a job.

**Charles:** *(He is quiet for a moment. He looks nervous.)* Okay. The truth is... I had to leave. I had no choice.

**Anna:** No choice? Why?

**Charles:** There are... bad men. A mafia. They are looking for me.

**Anna:** The mafia? Why? What did you do?

**Charles:** *(He shakes his head. His eyes are sad.)* I cannot say. I am sorry, Anna. I saw something. That is all I can say. I ran away. I cannot go back.

Anna looks at him. She sees he is honest and very afraid. She understands he has a secret.

**Anna:** Okay, Charles. You do not need to say more. You are safe here now.

**Charles:** Thank you for understanding.

Charles feels better. He has a new friend who knows a little of his truth. He feels hope for his new life in Sinea.

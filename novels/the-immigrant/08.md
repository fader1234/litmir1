Anna had a very big idea. She looked at Charles and said, "You are a good and honest man. You must be the new Mayor of Sinea!"

Charles was surprised. "Me? A Mayor? But I have no papers. I am an immigrant."

Anna smiled. "I have a friend. His name is Leo. He is a hacker. He can fix your papers on the computer."

They went to see Leo. Leo's room had many computers. Click, click, click. Leo typed very fast. "Done!" he said. "Now, Charles, you are legal. You have papers. You can be a candidate for Mayor."

Anna worked very hard. She organized the campaign. She put posters all over the city. The posters said: VOTE FOR CHARLES! HONESTY AND HOPE!

### Election Day

Election day was sunny. The main square in Sinea was full of people. There was a big stage. Two men wanted to be Mayor: Charles and Martin.

Martin went first. His face was angry. He spoke into the microphone.

"People of Sinea!" Martin said loudly. "Look at this man, Charles. He has no home. He has no country. He is an immigrant! Immigrants are a big problem. They take our jobs. They make our city dirty. They are illegal! We must say NO to immigrants! Vote for me, and I will make Sinea great again!"

Some people clapped, but not many. Many people looked unhappy.

Now it was Charles's turn. He walked to the microphone. He was nervous, but his voice was strong.

"Hello, people of Sinea," Charles began. "My name is Charles. Yes, I am an immigrant. I came here with nothing. I was afraid. I was hungry. But I found good people here. I found a friend. I found hope."

The crowd was quiet, listening.

"I have promises for you," Charles said. "First, I promise free buses for everybody! No more expensive tickets! Second, I promise to fight against the mafia! No more corruption! And third, I promise to respect all immigrants! In Sinea, no one is illegal! Everyone is important!"

The people started to clap. They smiled. They liked his promises.

Then Charles looked directly at Martin. His face became serious.

"And I have something to say to Mr. Martin," Charles said. His voice was clear and strong. "Martin, turn up the volume! Listen to me! I know everything about you. I know your dirty secret. I know about the slaves. I know about the illegal diamonds. We will find you! The police will find you!"

Martin's face was white. He was very afraid.

Charles smiled a little. Then he said one more thing. "And do you know something else, Martin? Your daughter's heart is mine!"

The crowd was silent for one second. Then… a BIG OVATION! People cheered, shouted, and clapped very loudly. "CHARLES! CHARLES! CHARLES!" they shouted.

Anna was on the stage. She looked at Charles and smiled. Her eyes were happy.

They counted the votes. The result was clear: Charles won! He was the new Mayor of Sinea!

The immigrants came to the stage. They hugged Charles. They danced and sang. "Now we have a Mayor who understands us!" they said.

Martin was very, very angry. His face was red like a tomato. "This is not over!" he shouted. But no one listened to him. He walked away, alone and angry.

Charles and Anna stood together on the stage. The sun was shining. The people were happy. Sinea had a new, honest Mayor. And Charles had not only a new city, but also a new friend and a new future.

Charles and Anna finished their dinner. They felt happy and comfortable together. Charles was grateful for his new friend.

"You know," Anna said, smiling proudly, "my father is a very important man in this city. He is a politician. He knows many people. Maybe he can help you. He can get you the right papers."

Charles listened. He felt a big wave of relief. This was the solution to all his problems! A powerful man could help him. He would be safe. He would be legal.

"Anna, thank you," Charles said. His voice was full of emotion. "This is… this is a dream. Yes, please, I accept your help."

Later that evening, Anna called her father. Charles listened to her side of the conversation.

"Papa, I have a new friend… His name is Charles… He is a very good man… He found the diamond from the museum and he returned it!… Yes, he is honest… But he has a problem. He has no papers… Can you help him? Can we see you tomorrow?"

Anna finished the call and smiled at Charles. "It is good. He wants to meet you tomorrow at his office. He is happy you returned the diamond."

Charles went to sleep that night with a happy heart. For the first time, he felt true hope. He thought, "Everything is going to change for me."

The next day, they walked to a big, important-looking building. It was her father's office. Charles was very hopeful. He smiled at Anna.

"This is a new start for me, Anna. Thank you," he said.

They walked into the office. It was a big room with a large desk. A man was sitting with his back to them. He was looking out of the window.

"Papa," Anna said. "We are here. This is my friend, Charles."

The man, Martin, turned his chair around. He looked at Charles.

In that moment, everything stopped.

Charles's face went white. He felt cold and sick. He knew this man. It was the man from his country! The corrupt politician he saw doing a terrible crime! The reason he had to run away!

Martin's face also changed. First, he was surprised. Then he was very, very angry. His eyes were wide with fear and fury.

"YOU!" Martin shouted, standing up quickly. "What are you doing with my daughter? What is this? Security! Police! Now!"

The door opened fast. Two big men in private security uniforms ran in. Behind them were two city police officers. They moved quickly to Charles and grabbed his arms. They were very strong.

Anna was shocked. "Papa! What are you doing?!" she screamed. "Stop! I don't understand! What is happening?"

Charles looked at Anna. His eyes were sad and scared. "Anna, he is the man!" Charles said, his voice shaking. "He is the reason I had to leave my country! He is a bad man!"

"Don't listen to him! He is a liar!" Martin shouted at his daughter. "He is dangerous!"

The police started to take Charles away. He did not fight. He was too shocked.

Anna was crying now. "Papa, no! Please, stop! Explain this to me! I don't understand anything! What is happening?"

But Martin did not look at his daughter. He watched as the police took Charles out of the room. The door closed. Anna was alone with her father, crying and confused. Charles was gone, and his hope was gone with him.

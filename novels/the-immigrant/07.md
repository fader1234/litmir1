Anna went to the police station. She asked, "Where is Charles? Why is he here?"

The police let her see Charles. Charles was sad. He decided to tell Anna everything.

"A long time ago, in my country," Charles said, "I saw a bad thing. I saw Martin. He had slaves. The slaves worked hard for diamonds. It was an illegal business. I saw it by accident. Then the diamond mafia started to terrorize me. They said, 'We will hurt you!' I was very afraid. So I left my country. I ran away. I did not know that Martin was the Mayor of Sinea!"

Anna listened. Her eyes were big. "My father? A bad man? With slaves?" She was shocked. But she believed Charles.

"I must help you," Anna said. "We must escape."

That night, Anna helped Charles escape from the police station. They ran away together.

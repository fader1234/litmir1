The policeman looks at Charles. "No papers? That is a big problem," he says. His voice is not friendly.

The other policeman looks at Charles's small bag. "A big problem needs a big solution," he says. He looks at Charles's pocket.

Charles is very scared. He understands what they want. He slowly takes his wallet out of his pocket. He has very little money. He gives some of it to the first policeman.

The policeman takes the money quickly. "Okay, go now. And do not make more problems," he says.

Charles walks away fast. His hands are shaking. He feels sad and angry. He finds the hotel 'Dark Sun'. It is an old building.

He goes inside. A man is at the reception desk.

"Hello, I need a room," Charles says.

The receptionist looks at him. "A room for one night?" he asks.

"Yes, please," Charles says.

"Okay. I need to see a document. Your passport or an ID card," the receptionist says.

Charles feels cold. "I... I do not have a document," he says quietly.

The receptionist shakes his head. "No document, no room. It is the rule," he says. Then he sees the small wallet in Charles's hand. He smiles a little. "But maybe... we can find a solution for your problem."

Charles understands again. He is tired of this. But he needs a place to sleep. He gives the receptionist some money. The man takes it and gives Charles a key.

Charles goes to his room. It is very small. He sits on the bed and looks at his wallet. There is not much money left. He thinks, "I cannot give money to everyone. If I do this, I will lose all my money. I must do something different. Tomorrow, I must find a solution."

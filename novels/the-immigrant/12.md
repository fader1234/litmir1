It was very early in the morning. The sky was grey and pink. Anna was waiting in front of the police station.

The door opened. Charles came out. He looked tired, but he was free.

Anna ran to him. They held each other for a long time. They did not say anything. They did not need to.

"Inspector Vega told me everything," Charles said at last. "The video. The port. Your father." He looked into her eyes. "Anna, I am so sorry."

"Do not be sorry," Anna said. "You did not make this choice. I did. And I am going to live with it."

* * *

One week later, the courtroom was full. There were journalists, workers from the road, immigrants, teachers from Anna's school. Mr. Bell was there too, in his best suit.

The judge was a tall woman with grey hair. She looked at the papers on her desk for a long time. Then she looked at the people.

First, the two policemen spoke. They did not smile now. They told the judge everything. The Mayor paid them to steal the diamond. They put it under the road. They took money from immigrants every night. And they sent the immigrants to the hotel 'Dark Sun', because the receptionist gave them half of his bribes.

The people in the courtroom were angry. "Shame! Shame!" they shouted.

"Silence!" said the judge.

Then it was Charles's turn. He stood up and told his story. The mines in the north of his country. The workers with no money, no freedom, no names. The night when he saw Martin there. The mafia. The ship. His first night in Sinea.

When he finished, the courtroom was very quiet.

Then Inspector Vega stood up. "Your Honor, I have news from Charles's country. Yesterday, the police there closed the mines. Three hundred workers are free. They have gone home to their families. This has happened because one man found a diamond and gave it back."

Many people in the courtroom were crying. Anna held Charles's hand.

The judge read her decision.

"Martin, the former Mayor of Sinea: ten years in prison for slavery, for the theft of the diamond and for corruption. The two policemen: four years in prison. The receptionist of the hotel 'Dark Sun': two years in prison."

She turned the page.

"Leo, the hacker. You made false papers. That is a crime. But you also helped the investigation. So, no prison. You must do two hundred hours of community work. You are going to teach computers to children at a school."

Leo looked at Anna. Anna smiled. "At my school," she said quietly.

Then the judge looked at Charles.

"And now, Charles. This is the most difficult part," she said. "You are a brave and honest man. This city owes you a lot. But your papers were false. So the election was not legal. You cannot be the Mayor of Sinea."

People started to shout. "No! He is our Mayor! Charles! Charles!"

Charles stood up and lifted his hand. The people stopped.

"The judge is right," he said. "A Mayor must follow the law. I said that to Martin. I must say it to myself too."

The judge nodded. She almost smiled.

"There will be a new election in one year," she said. "And I have one more decision. In his country, the diamond mafia wants to hurt this man. He is a very important witness. So this court gives him asylum. Real papers. Legal papers." She looked at Charles over her glasses. "Not made on a computer."

The people jumped and cheered. Charles could not speak. He looked at the paper in his hands. There was his photo. His name. And one word, in big letters: RESIDENT.

For the first time in his life, Charles was not afraid of the police.

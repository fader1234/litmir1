Charles gets off a ship. He arrives in the city of Sinea. The city is big and noisy. He does not know anyone. He does not know where to go.

He is tired and hungry. He has a small bag with his clothes. He looks around. Many people walk fast. They do not look at him.

He stops a woman.

"Hello, my name is Charles. I am lost in the city. Can you help me find a good place to sleep?" he asks.

The woman looks at him. She is kind.

"Yes, you can go to the hotel 'Dark Sun'," she says.

"Thank you," Charles says.

He walks to the hotel. The streets are dark now. He is afraid.

Then the police stop him.

"Stop! Who are you?" a policeman asks.

Charles is nervous. He does not understand.

"My name is Charles. I am going to the hotel," he says.

The police look at his bag.

"Show me your papers," the policeman says.

Charles does not have papers. He is in trouble.

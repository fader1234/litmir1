The next day, Charles goes out. He needs to find a job.

He looks for a job all day. Finally, he finds one. It is a bad job. He works with asphalt on the road. The work is very hard. The sun is hot. Charles is tired.

One day, Charles is digging a hole in the ground. Suddenly, he sees something in the dirt. It is a stone. But it is not a normal stone. It is very bright. It is a beautiful turquoise color. It is a diamond!

He picks it up. He is surprised. He thinks, "Maybe this diamond can give me money. This is good for me!"

Later, a coworker reads the news. He shows it to Charles.

"Look," the coworker says. "A diamond was stolen from the city museum! The police and the special police are searching for the robber. They want to find the diamond."

Charles reads the news. His heart beats fast. He is nervous. He looks at the diamond in his pocket.

He doesn't know what to do. He thinks, "Maybe this is the stolen diamond? I am in trouble."

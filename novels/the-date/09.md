The room was still full of laughter, but Frank felt a cold wave of anger rising in his chest. The relief of seeing Tom alive was now mixed with the sting of betrayal. For two years, he had lived with grief, with a burning need for justice. He had turned his life into a hunt, risked his safety, and faced dangerous, unstable people—all for a game.

The laughter died down as Frank stood up, the ropes now loose at his feet. He looked at his brother, his expression unreadable.

“A game, Tom?” Frank’s voice was low, but it cut through the room. “You faked your death. For two years, I thought you were murdered. I stopped living. Everything I did was to find your killer.”

Tom’s smile faltered. “Frank, come on. It was just a performance art piece! My big break! I knew you’d investigate—you’re the best detective I know. I wanted to see if you could solve it.”

“Solve what?” Frank’s voice rose. “A lie? You used me. You used all of them.” He gestured to the women, who now looked uncomfortable, shifting on their feet. “You made me distrust every person I met. You made me see killers in shadows.”

“We were acting!” Clara protested weakly.

“You made me live in the past,” Frank continued, ignoring her, his eyes locked on Tom. “While you were off… what? Taking acting classes? Did you ever think about what it was doing to me? To Mom? She still visits your grave every Sunday, Tom.”

The color drained from Tom’s face. The room was utterly silent. The playful, theatrical atmosphere had vanished, replaced by the heavy weight of a very real hurt.

“I… I didn’t think…” Tom stammered.

“That’s the problem,” Frank said. He picked up his coat from the back of the chair. “You never do.”

He walked toward the door. No one moved to stop him.

“Frank, wait!” Tom called out, his voice desperate.

Frank paused at the threshold but didn’t turn around.

“I’m sorry,” Tom said, the words sounding hollow in the large, dark room. “It was stupid. I’m so sorry.”

Frank took a deep breath, his hand on the doorknob. Without another word, he stepped out into the cold night, leaving the staged mystery and his resurrected brother behind.

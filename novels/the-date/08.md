Mark was tied to the chair.
Vanessa played her scary violin music.
Then,she stopped.

"Good!" she said. "Now, it is time for the meeting."
She took her phone and made a call.

"Hello? It's me. Yes, he is here. You can all come in now."

Mark was confused. "Who is coming?" he asked.

Vanessa just smiled. "You will see."

The door opened. One by one, the women walked in.
First Clara,then Veronica, then Nadia, then Susan, then Rosa.
They all stood in front of Mark.They all looked at him.

Mark could not believe his eyes.
"Why...why are you all here?" he asked. He was very afraid.

Clara spoke first. "You asked me about my mother's birthday. It was very rude! I wanted to teach you a lesson."

Veronica laughed. "I love crazy games! This is the best game!"

Nadia looked angry. "You asked too many questions. A man should be quiet and handsome."

Susan sighed. "You didn't listen to my stories. That is very, very bad."

Rosa took a paper from her pocket. "This is a contract. You broke the rules of dating. You must pay."

Mark's head was spinning. He did not understand.
All these women...because of bad dates? It was crazy!

Suddenly, another person walked out of the dark room.
It was a man.

The man smiled. "Hi, Mark," he said. "Or must I say... Frank?"

Mark looked at the man's face.
He knew that face.

It was his brother.

"Tom?!" Mark shouted. "You are alive! But... why?"

Tom laughed. "I am sorry, Frank. This was a big game. I wanted to be an actor! So, I asked my actress friends to help me. We made a story for you."

Mark looked at all the women. They were all smiling now.

"You... you are all actors?" Mark asked.

"Yes!" said Clara. "It was a fun job."

Tom walked closer. "I am sorry I made you sad. But you are a very good detective brother! You found all the 'suspects'."

Mark didn't know if he was angry or happy.
His brother was alive!

"But... why the rope?" Mark asked.

Vanessa laughed. "For the drama! Every good story needs a big finish!"

Everyone laughed. Mark laughed too. He was free, and his brother was alive. It was the best surprise.

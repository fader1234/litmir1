My next date was with a woman named Rosa.
Her job was a lawyer.
I thought,"This woman is smart. She can be dangerous."

We met at a small coffee shop.
She wore a black suit.
She looked very serious.

"Hello, Mark," she said.
"Tell me about your life.I will analyse it."

"Analyse it?" I asked.

"Yes. Like in a court," she said.
"Now,our relationship. What are your intentions?"
She looked at me like I was in court.

"My... intentions?" I said.

"Yes. Is this a friendly meeting?
Or do you want a long-term relationship?
I need to know the rules."

I did not know what to say.
I just wanted to know if she killed my brother.

"I... want to know you," I said.

"That is not a good answer," she said.
"It is too general.The court does not like general answers."

She took a small notebook.
"Exhibit A,"she said. "You are nervous.
This is not good for a first date."

"Exhibit B," she continued.
"You did not bring flowers.
This shows a lack of romantic intention."

I was very confused.
This was not a date;it was a trial!

"Where were you two years ago?" I asked quickly.

She stopped writing.
She looked at me with sharp eyes.

"That is an interesting question," she said.
"Why do you ask?Do you have evidence against me?"

"No! I... I am just curious," I said.

"I object!" she said loudly.
"That question is not relevant."

I finished my coffee fast.
"I have to go,"I said.

"The court is not happy with this," Rosa said.
"But you are free to go.For now."

I left the coffee shop.
My head hurt.
Rosa was not the killer.
But she was the most difficult date.

### Fun Activity: "In the Court!"

Play a simple role-play. One student is the Lawyer (Rosa), one is the Client (Mark).
Lawyer asks simple questions: "What is your name?" "How old are you?" "Do you like coffee?"
Client answers. Then they switch. This practices simple Q&A in a fun context related to the story.

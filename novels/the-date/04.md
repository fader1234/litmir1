Mark was tired. He opened Tinder again. Suddenly, a message appeared:

"Hi, Mark. Let’s meet tonight. —Nadia"

Mark was surprised. Women never asked first. Maybe she’s the killer! he thought.

They met at a romantic restaurant. Nadia had short blonde hair and a sweet smile. But her eyes were cold.

"Tell me about yourself," Mark said.

"I like kind men," Nadia replied. "But one man made me cry. He had brown hair and a nice smile... like yours."

Mark’s heart jumped. That sounds like Tom!

"What happened to him?" Mark asked.

Nadia looked angry. "Why so many questions?"

"I’m just curious," Mark said.

Nadia sipped her wine. "Do you live alone?"

"Yes," Mark lied.

"Good," she whispered.

Mark felt nervous. She’s suspicious!

Suddenly, Nadia kissed him. But then she pulled away quickly.

"I have to go!" she said, and ran out.

Mark touched his lips. Did she feel Tom’s kiss? Or... did I forget to brush my teeth?

He didn’t know. But one thing was clear—Nadia was hiding something.

Mark went home. He was very tired.
He sat at his table with a notebook.
He thought about all the women.

First, Clara. She was boring.
She only asked about money and dogs.
Not the killer.

Second, Veronica. She was crazy and dangerous.
But the police took her.
Not the killer.

Third, Nadia. She was suspicious.
She kissed him and ran away.
But why?He didn't know.
Maybe not the killer.

Fourth, Susan. She talked too much about her ex-boyfriends.
She was not dangerous,just very annoying.
Not the killer.

Fifth, Rosa. The lawyer.
She analysed everything like a court.
Difficult,but not a killer.

He put his head in his hands.
"Who killed you,Tom?" he said to the empty room.

Then, his phone buzzed.
A new message on Tinder.

Username: Vanessa_Violin
Message:"Hello, Mark. Your profile is interesting. Come to my house tonight. I will play the violin for you."

Mark's heart beat fast.
A date at her house?This is strange.
But maybe she is the one!

He wrote back: "I will come."

Vanessa's house was big and old.
She opened the door.
She had long white hair and a smile that was too big.

"Welcome, Mark!" she said. "Come in."
The house was dark.There were many violins on the walls.

"Sit here," she said, pointing to a chair.
"I will play a song for you."

She started to play the violin.
The music was beautiful but also sad.
Mark felt very sleepy.

His eyes closed.

Mark woke up.
He could not move.
His hands and feet were tied to the chair with rope.

"Ah! You are awake!" Vanessa said.
She was standing in front of him with her violin.

"Good morning, dear," she said, but it was night.
"Now I will play a concert for you.Hahaha!"

She was really crazy.

"Let me go!" Mark shouted.

"No, no, no," she said, shaking her head.
"You must listen to my violin.It is a special song for my... special guests."

She put the violin under her chin.
The music started again.
This time,it was not beautiful.
It was scary.

Mark was afraid.
He needed to escape.

### Askingforhelp

The waiting room has three chairs, a desk, and a clock that makes a loud noise. Alex sits beside Olga and tries not to watch the second hand. They came here for help, but now he wishes that he had a simpler story. The officer at the desk asks for his name.

“Alex. I need to find out what to do about my documents.”

“Are they lost?”

“Not exactly. They don't exist yet.”

The officer puts down his pen. Alex explains the chip, the machine, and the year

3024. The officer thinks it may be a joke, but Olga describes the woman in the restaurant. She is careful to say only what she saw.

“I can't put a story about time travel on an ordinary form,” the officer says. “Do you have anything with your name on it?”

Alex remembers a small blue recorder inside the machine. It stores his name, pictures from the journey, and information from his chip. It will not be a passport, but it may help him explain. He tells Olga where to find it.

“Behind the seat, in the top drawer,” he says. “The blue button opens it. You don't need to start the machine.”

He gives her the key and the notebook. Olga looks at the page about HOME and RETURN. She also sees the warning that Alex underlined that afternoon.

“Is it dangerous?” she asks.

“It's old. I checked the power before dinner, but please just bring the recorder.”

Olga agrees. Alex offers to come, but she asks him to stay and finish explaining his situation. Before she leaves, she puts the restaurant receipt beside his hand. His first paid dinner in 2024 seems like something worth keeping.

At ten to ten, Olga leaves the station. She knows the alley because Alex showed her its entrance earlier in the week. He watches her through the window until she turns the corner.

The officer gives Alex the address of an office where he can ask for further help. There is nothing more they can do at the station tonight. Alex thanks him, but asks to stay in the waiting room until Olga comes back.

“Of course,” the officer says. “Would you like some water?”

Alex accepts the cup. He looks at the empty chair and remembers the other woman's words. For the first time since arriving in the past, he is not thinking about finding someone to take home. He wants Olga to return safely to hers.

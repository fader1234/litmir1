## to the Future Baby

![](novels/a-man-from-the-future/img/03.jpg)

### Arbatstreet

Alex spends the morning walking through Moscow. He reads street signs, watches people cross the road, and stops to listen to a musician. By the time he reaches Arbat Street, he feels almost at home. This confidence does not last long.

Near a bookshop, a young woman is finishing a phone call. Alex hears her say, “Yes, this is Svetlana.” She puts her phone away, and he walks straight toward her.

“Hello, Svetlana. I'm Alex. I like you.”

She looks surprised. “Do I know you?”

“Not yet. Do you want to come with me to the future, baby?”

Alex remembers the last word from an old romantic movie. In the movie, the woman smiles. Svetlana does not smile at all.

“No. Please leave me alone.”

She walks toward the bookshop. Alex follows her for a few steps because he wants to explain. He thinks the problem is his strange story, and that more words will help.

“Wait! I really have a machine.”

Svetlana turns around. “Stop following me!”

Her voice is loud enough to bring the bookseller to the door. Alex stops at once. The bookseller stands beside Svetlana and asks if she is all right.

“I just want to talk,” Alex says.

“And she doesn't,” the bookseller answers. “You need to listen.”

For a moment, nobody speaks. Alex looks at Svetlana's face and finally understands that she is afraid. He moves away from the doorway so that she can enter the shop.

“I'm sorry,” he says. “I won't follow you.”

Svetlana goes inside. Alex stays where he is until the door closes, then walks in the opposite direction. He does not try to explain again.

On a bench, he takes Olga's card from his pocket. He thinks about the movie and his carefully chosen words. He knows almost nothing about Svetlana, but he asked her to leave her whole life behind. Then, when she said no, he did not stop.

At a quarter to one, he gets up and starts walking back to the café. His adventure feels less like a love story now. He hopes Olga will still be there, because he needs to tell someone what happened.

2. Alex _______ understand her at first.

3. "I _______ want to talk," Svetlana says.

4. The people outside _______ know Alex's story.

5. He _______ follow her after he apologizes.

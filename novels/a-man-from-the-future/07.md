### Aneveningtogether

At eight that evening, Alex visits the alley to check the machine. The power light is green. He saves his present position and the time: June 27, 2024, 8:00 p.m. Then he locks the door and walks to the restaurant with the notebook in his pocket.

Olga arrives at a quarter past eight. She is wearing a blue dress, and her hair is tied back. Alex almost forgets to say hello. She touches his arm and asks whether people in the future usually stare instead of speaking.

“Only when they can't find the right words,” he says.

Over dinner, Alex tries to cut a large piece of bread. It slips off his plate and lands beside Olga's fork. She picks it up before it can escape again.

“Is this another machine?” she asks.

“No. That one doesn't obey me either.”

They laugh, and Alex relaxes. He tells her about the gray workroom and the food machines. Then Olga asks a practical question: what will he do about his missing documents? His little chip cannot replace a passport here.

“I don't know,” he admits. “I can't keep pretending that it doesn't matter.”

They agree to visit the nearby police station after dinner and ask where to begin. Alex is nervous, but he is tired of hiding his problem. Olga offers to go with him.

At twenty-five past eight, the restaurant door opens. A woman in a blue dress comes straight toward their table. There is a dark mark on her sleeve, and her hair is loose. Otherwise, she looks exactly like Olga.

The woman takes Alex's arm. “Come with me. I need to talk to you.”

Then she sees Olga sitting opposite him and stops. Olga stands, turns pale, and falls back into her chair. Alex stays beside her while the waiter brings a glass of water.

“Wait,” the other woman says quietly. “I'm sorry. Please stay with her.”

“Who are you?” Alex asks.

“You'll understand tonight. Wait for me at the station.”

She leaves before he can ask another question. Olga is still looking at the door, but the color is returning to her face. She tells the waiter that she feels better and takes a slow drink of water.

“I don't have a twin,” she says to Alex.

He takes out the notebook and shows her the warning about meeting your earlier self. Olga reads it, then looks at her clean sleeve. She does not know what to believe, but she cannot dismiss his story so easily now.

They leave the restaurant after nine. On the way to the station, Olga reaches for Alex's hand. He holds it carefully and keeps walking beside her. Neither of them says much.

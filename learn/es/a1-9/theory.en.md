## A dress for the date

The novel «La gran conspiración»: Natalia, a cardiologist from Moscow, gets messages from two Argentinians on the same day. Tonight she has a date with Luis, the artist — so she goes shopping.

> — Buenas tardes. **¿Qué desea?**{Can I help you?}
> — Hola. **Busco un vestido**{I'm looking for a dress} para **esta noche**{tonight}.
> — ¿**De qué color**{What colour}?
> — **Rojo**{Red}… o **negro**{black}.
> — **Este vestido**{This dress} rojo es muy elegante. ¿Qué **talla**{size} tiene?
> — La 42. **¿Puedo probármelo?**{Can I try it on?}
> — Claro. **El probador**{The fitting room} está **allí**{over there}.
> — Me queda **perfecto**{perfect}. **¿Cuánto cuesta?**{How much is it?}
> — **Doce mil rublos**{Twelve thousand roubles}.
> — ¡Uf! **Es caro**{It's expensive}… ¿Y **aquel**{that one over there} negro?
> — **Aquel** cuesta **seis mil quinientos**{six thousand five hundred}. **Está rebajado**{It's on sale}.
> — **Me lo llevo**{I'll take it}.

## 1. How much is it?

- **¿Cuánto cuesta** este vestido? — How much is this dress?
- **¿Cuánto cuestan** estos zapatos? — How much are these shoes?
- **Cuesta** veinte euros. — It costs twenty euros.
- **Es caro / barato.** — It's expensive / cheap.

*Costar* changes o → ue (unit 7): *cuesta, cuestan*.

## 2. Numbers from 100

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 cien | 101 ciento uno | 200 doscientos | 300 trescientos |
| 400 cuatrocientos | 500 **quinientos** | 600 seiscientos | 700 **setecientos** |
| 800 ochocientos | 900 **novecientos** | 1000 mil | 2000 dos mil |

- **Cien** — exactly a hundred; **ciento** — above it: *ciento veinte* (120).
- Hundreds agree in gender: *doscient**os** rublos, doscient**as** personas*.
- **Mil** doesn't change: *dos mil, doce mil*. 1,000,000 — *un millón*.
- *Y* goes only between tens and units: *treinta **y** cinco*, but *ciento cinco* (no *y*).

## 3. Colours

| Español | English | Español | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| rojo, -a | red | blanco, -a | white |
| negro, -a | black | amarillo, -a | yellow |
| azul | blue | verde | green |
| gris | grey | marrón | brown |
| rosa | pink | naranja | orange |

Colours are adjectives: they go **after** the noun and agree: *un vestido roj**o**, una falda roj**a**, unos zapatos roj**os***. Colours ending in *-e* or a consonant only change for number: *verde → verdes, azul → azules*.

## 5. This, that, that one over there

| Where? | masc. | fem. | plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| near me — "this" | **este** vestido | **esta** falda | **estos** / **estas** |
| near you — "that" | **ese** abrigo | **esa** camisa | **esos** / **esas** |
| far away — "that over there" | **aquel** bolso | **aquella** chaqueta | **aquellos** / **aquellas** |

Without a noun — "this / that thing": **esto**, **eso**. *¿Qué es esto?* — What is this?

## 6. Polite phrases in a shop

- **Quisiera** ver ese abrigo. — I'd like to see that coat. (*quisiera* is more polite than *quiero*)
- **¿Tiene** otra talla? — Do you have another size?
- **Me queda grande / pequeño.** — It's too big / too small for me.
- **¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta?** — Can I pay by card?
- **Me lo llevo.** — I'll take it.

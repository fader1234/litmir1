## Luisův plán

In «Předtucha» Kazimír’s savages have taken Naše utopie. The council meets in the armoury at Karlštejn, and Luis scatters beans over the map: he has a plan (chapter 16). Later, before the great battle, he shows Robert his drawings of the dam (chapter 22). Here is a simplified scene based on these chapters.

> — **Divochů je tři sta dvanáct**{There are three hundred and twelve savages}. **Nás je sto čtyřicet**{There are a hundred and forty of us}.
> — **To je sebevražda!**{That’s suicide!}
> — **Nebudeme bojovat tam, kde to čekají**{We won’t fight where they expect it}. **Půjdeme tunelem**{We’ll go through the tunnel} do prázdného města.
> — **Vojta jim pošle zprávu**{Vojta will send them a message}, — Jana realises.
> — Ano. **Napíše jim**{He’ll write to them}, že se zítra v noci stěhujeme na jih.
> — **Co když nám Kazimír nebude věřit?**{What if Kazimír doesn’t believe us?}
> — **Bude věřit.**{He will.} **Když mu napíšeme pravdu, uvěří i lži.**{If we write him the truth, he’ll believe the lie too.}
> — **Kdy jsi tohle všechno vymyslel?**{When did you come up with all this?} — Robert asks.
> — **Celou zimu jsem na tom pracoval.**{I worked on it all winter.} **Jana mi pořád brala tužku.**{Jana kept taking my pencil away.}

## 1. Two aspects: one action, two verbs

Most Czech verbs come in **pairs**. The **imperfective** verb (nedokonavý) describes a process or a repeated action; the **perfective** verb (dokonavý) describes one completed action with a result.

| Imperfective | Perfective | English |
|---|---|---|
| **dělat** | **udělat** | to do, make |
| **psát** (píšu) | **napsat** (napíšu) | to write |
| **číst** (čtu) | **přečíst** (přečtu) | to read |
| **kupovat** (kupuju) | **koupit** (koupím) | to buy |
| **otevírat** | **otevřít** (otevřu) | to open |
| **zavírat** | **zavřít** (zavřu) | to close |
| **říkat** | **říct** (řeknu) | to say, tell |
| **brát** (beru) | **vzít** (vezmu) | to take |

Pairs are formed with a prefix (*u-dělat, na-psat, pře-číst*), with a change of suffix (*kupovat → koupit, otevírat → otevřít*) or with a completely different verb (*brát → vzít, říkat → říct*). Learn every new verb together with its partner.

English has no real equivalent: "I wrote" can be *psal jsem* (I was writing, I used to write) or *napsal jsem* (I wrote it, it’s done). Czech always makes you choose.

## 2. Process or result

| Process (how long?) | Result (done!) |
|---|---|
| *Luis **psal** dopis celý večer.* — Luis was writing a letter all evening. | *Luis **napsal** dopis za pět minut.* — Luis wrote a letter in five minutes. |
| *Celou zimu jsem na tom **pracoval**.* — I worked on it all winter. | *Kdy jsi tohle všechno **vymyslel**?* — When did you think all this up? |
| *Dlouho **četla** zprávu.* — She was reading the message for a long time. | *Konečně ji **přečetla**.* — Finally she read it (to the end). |

Signal words for the imperfective: **celý den, celou zimu, dlouho, dvě hodiny**. For the perfective: **za pět minut** (in five minutes), **konečně, už, hned**.

Trap: "for two hours" → imperfective (*psal dvě hodiny*); "in two hours" → perfective (*napsal to za dvě hodiny*).

In chapter 29 Lucie pulls the halberd with all her strength and the sluice gate finally moves. Luis tells her: *«Ty jsi to **otevřela**. Sama.»* — You opened it. Alone. A result, so perfective.

## 3. Many times or once

| Repeated | Once |
|---|---|
| *Luis tam **chodil** každé ráno a **měřil** hladinu.* | *Pátého dne **řekl** Tomášovi: „Ještě dva metry.“* |
| *Jana mi pořád **brala** tužku.* | *Luis **vzal** hrst fazolí.* — Luis took a handful of beans. |
| *Každý týden **kupuju** chleba.* | *Dnes **koupím** chleba i sýr.* |

Signal words for repetition: **každý den, každé ráno, pořád, často, vždycky, obvykle**. English "used to" or "kept doing" is a clear sign of the imperfective.

## 4. The future: BUDU DĚLAT or UDĚLÁM?

The imperfective future is **budu + infinitive** (unit 10). The perfective has no present tense at all — its **present form already means the future**:

| | Imperfective | Perfective |
|---|---|---|
| now | **dělám** — I’m doing | — (no present!) |
| future | **budu dělat** — I’ll be doing | **udělám** — I’ll do (it) |
| past | **dělal jsem** — I was doing | **udělal jsem** — I did (it) |

- *Zítra **budu** celý den **psát**.* — Tomorrow I’ll be writing all day.
- *Večer ti **napíšu**.* — I’ll write to you in the evening.
- *Vojta jim **pošle** zprávu.* — Vojta will send them a message.

Two classic mistakes: *napíšu* does **not** mean "I write" — it means "I will write" ("I’m writing now" = *Teď píšu*). And **never** *budu udělat*: *budu* only goes with imperfective verbs.

Going verbs, as you know from A1, use a prefix: *půjdu, pojedu* — *«Půjdeme tunelem do prázdného města.»*

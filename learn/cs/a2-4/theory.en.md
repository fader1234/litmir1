## S Lucií a s Janou

In chapter 26 of «Předtucha» («Noc ohňů») the savages break through the south gate of Naše utopie at night. In the gap stands Lucie, alone, with an old halberd that once hung on the school wall as a decoration. Luis runs to her with an axe, and they fight back to back. Here is a simplified version of the scene.

> — **Brána! Prolomili bránu!**{The gate! They’ve broken the gate!}
> — Kde je Lucie?
> — **Stojí mezi křídly brány**{She’s standing between the wings of the gate}. **Sama, s halapartnou.**{Alone, with a halberd.}
> — **Kdo dál? No tak!**{Who’s next? Come on!} — Lucie shouts.
> — **Jsem tady, se sekerou!**{I’m here, with an axe!} — Luis stands **zády k jejím zádům**{with his back to her back}.
> — **Ahoj**{Hi}, — says Lucie without turning round.
> — Ahoj. **Jako v Holešovicích.**{Like in Holešovice.}
> — **Kdo je s námi?**{Who’s with us?}
> — **Tomáš s kladivem a Petr s mečem.**{Tomáš with a hammer and Petr with a sword.}
> — **A kdo zpívá nad bránou?**{And who’s singing above the gate?}
> — **Robert. Stojí na hradbě, sám, bez zbraně.**{Robert. He’s standing on the wall, alone, unarmed.}
> — **Stát s tebou zády k sobě… to je naše.**{Standing back to back with you… that’s ours.} — Lucie says in the morning.

## 1. Instrumental endings (7. pád)

The instrumental answers **kým? čím?** — (with) whom? (with) what? It expresses company, a tool or means, and a role.

| Gender | Nominative | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| masc. | Robert, meč, divoch | s Robert**em**, s meč**em** | s divoch**y**, s muž**i** |
| neut. | kladivo, auto | s kladiv**em**, aut**em** | s aut**y** |
| fem. in -a | Jana, halapartna, sekera | s Jan**ou**, s halapartn**ou** | se žen**ami** |
| fem. in -e / consonant | Lucie, noc | s Luci**í**, s noc**í** | s ulic**emi**, s kost**mi** |

Adjectives: *s velk**ým** mečem*, *s velk**ou** sekerou*, *s velk**ými** zbraněmi*.

Learn these exceptions: **s lidmi** (with people), **s dětmi** (with children), **rukama, nohama, očima** (with hands, feet, eyes): *bránili se holýma **rukama*** — they defended themselves with their bare hands.

## 2. S kým? S čím? — with whom? with what?

The preposition **s** (before *s, z, š, ž* and hard clusters: **se**):

- *Lucie stojí v bráně **s halapartnou**.*
- *Luis přiběhl **se sekerou**.*
- *Jana sedí ve věži **s tabletem**.* In chapter 16 Jana tells Luis: *«Já budu tady. **S tabletem.**»* — I’ll be here. With the tablet.

## 3. Čím? — tool and transport (no preposition!)

| Example | English |
|---|---|
| *Jedeme **vlakem** / **autem**.* | We go by train / by car. |
| *Píšu **tužkou**.* | I write with a pencil. |
| *Lucie bojuje **halapartnou**.* | Lucie fights with a halberd. |
| *Robert je přikryl **kabátem**.* | Robert covered them with his coat. |

Trap: English uses "with" for both company and tools, but Czech doesn’t. A tool or a vehicle takes the bare instrumental: *píšu tužkou*, *jedu autem*. *Jedu s autem* would mean "I’m travelling together with a car". *S* + instrumental is for company: *jdu s Janou*.

## 4. BÝT and STÁT SE + instrumental

Roles and professions are often in the instrumental:

- *Luis se **stal velitelem**.* — Luis became the commander.
- *Robert je **zakladatelem** Naší utopie.* — Robert is the founder of Naše utopie.
- *Lucie byla **bojovnicí**.* — Lucie was a fighter.

With **být** you can also use the nominative: *Tomáš je kovář* = *Tomáš je kovářem*. With **stát se** (to become) only the instrumental is possible.

## 5. Where? PŘED, ZA, NAD, POD, MEZI + instrumental

| Preposition | Example based on the novel |
|---|---|
| **před** — in front of | *Falešná zeď stála sto metrů **před hradbami**.* |
| **za** — behind | *Voda **za hrází** stoupala každý den.* |
| **nad** — above | *Robert zpíval na hradbě **nad bránou**.* |
| **pod** — under | ***Pod severní zdí** vede starý tunel.* |
| **mezi** — between, among | *Lucie stála **mezi křídly** brány.* |

These prepositions take the instrumental for **where?** (*pod stolem* — under the table). For **where to?** they take the accusative (*pod stůl* — (put it) under the table).

## 6. Pronouns

| | with a preposition | without |
|---|---|---|
| já | **se mnou** | mnou |
| ty | **s tebou** | tebou |
| on / ono | **s ním** | jím |
| ona | **s ní** | jí |
| my | **s námi** | námi |
| vy | **s vámi** | vámi |
| oni | **s nimi** | jimi |

*Pojď **s námi**!* — Come with us! *Stát **s tebou** zády k sobě.* — To stand back to back with you. By the way, *zády* is an instrumental too (from *záda* — back): literally "with backs to each other".

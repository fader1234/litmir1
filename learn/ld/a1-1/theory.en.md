## Meet Natalia and her brothers

The novel «Premonision»: during the war, Natalia, a cellist, hides her three younger brothers — Maksim, Artem and Denis — in an underground shelter. Lingwa de Planeta (Lidepla) is built from words of the world’s biggest languages: Russian, English, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, Arabic…

Hover over a word (or tap it) to see its translation.

> — **Me es**{I am} Natalia. **Me es violonchelista**{I’m a cellist}.
> — **Natalia, yu es la pyu hao violonchelista in oli mundo**{Natalia, you’re the best cellist in the whole world}!
> — **Non**{No}, **me bu es la pyu hao**{I’m not the best}. **Bat me es muy felise**{But I’m very happy}.
> — **Me go kun yu**{I’ll go with you} — **Maksim respond-te**{Maksim answered}.
> — **Non**{No}. **Yu mus resta hir**{You must stay here} kun Denis e Artem.
> — **Me ve reveni**{I will come back}.

## 1. Greetings and goodbyes

| Lingwa de Planeta | English |
|---|---|
| Salam! | Hello! |
| Hao dia! | Good day! |
| Hao nocha! | Good night! |
| Ya / Non | Yes / No |
| Hao | Good, OK |

You’ll recognise many Lidepla words: **hao** (good) comes from Chinese, **dia** (day) from Spanish, **nocha** (night) from Spanish and Russian, **mus** (must) from German and English.

## 2. Introducing yourself

- **Me es Natalia.** — I’m Natalia.
- **May nam es Natalia.** — My name is Natalia.
- **Kwo es nam de yu?** — What’s your name? (What is the name of you?)
- **Yu es hao? — Ya, me es hao.** — Are you OK? — Yes, I’m fine.

## 3. Subject pronouns

| Lingwa de Planeta | English |
|---|---|
| me | I |
| yu | you |
| lu / ela | he / she |
| it | it |
| nu | we |
| li | they |

Possessives: **may** (my), **nuy** (our), **suy** (one’s own): *may karim* — my dear, *nuy dom* — our home. The book in the novel, «Nuy Utopia», means "Our Utopia".

## 4. The verb “es”

Lidepla verbs **don’t change for person**: *me es, yu es, li es*.

| Person | ES | Example |
|---|---|---|
| me | **es** | Me es Natalia. |
| yu | **es** | Yu es la pyu hao! |
| lu / ela | **es** | Ela es violonchelista. |
| nu | **es** | Nu es frates. |
| li | **es** | Li es in la refugio. |

Tense is shown by small particles. Past — **-te**: *ela es-te* (she was), *ela shwo-te* (she said). Future — **ve**: *me ve reveni* (I will come back). Negation — **bu**: *me bu es* (I am not).

## 5. Where are you from?

- **Me es de Rusia.** — I’m from Russia.
- **Wo yu jivi?** — Where do you live?
- **Me jivi in un refugio.** — I live in a shelter.



## 6. Pronunciation

| Letters | Sound | Example |
|---|---|---|
| sh | "sh" | shwo (сказать / say) |
| j | "j" as in *jam* | jan, jiva |
| y | "y" | yu, ya |
| kw | "kw" | kwo |
| ay, oy | "eye", "oy" | may, koysa |

## 7. Good to know

Lidepla has no gender, no cases and no conjugation — but it does have articles: **la** (the) and **un** (a): *la mundo* — the world, *un dia* — one day.

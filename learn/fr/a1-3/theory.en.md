## Oxana’s family

Oxana is Sasha’s friend and colleague in the novel «La voix». Let’s imagine that during the lunch break she shows Sasha photos of her family.

> — Regarde, **c’est ma famille**{this is my family} ! **Voici mon père**{Here’s my dad}, **il est médecin**{he’s a doctor}.
> — **Il est grand**{He’s tall} ! Et cette femme **brune**{dark-haired} ?
> — **C’est ma mère**{That’s my mum}. **Elle est très gentille**{She’s very kind}.
> — **Tu as des frères et sœurs ?**{Do you have brothers and sisters?}
> — **J’ai un frère**{I have a brother}, Ivan. **Il a vingt ans**{He’s twenty} et **il est étudiant**{he’s a student}. Et toi ?
> — Moi, **je n’ai pas de frère**{I don’t have a brother}. **J’ai une petite sœur**{I have a little sister}.
> — Et **dans ton appartement**{in your flat}, **il y a**{is there} un chat ?
> — Non… **il n’y a pas de chat**{there’s no cat}. Mais **il y a beaucoup de livres**{but there are lots of books} !

## 1. Family

| Français | English | Français | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| le père | father | la mère | mother |
| les parents | parents | les enfants | children |
| le frère | brother | la sœur | sister |
| le fils | son | la fille | daughter; girl |
| le grand-père | grandfather | la grand-mère | grandmother |
| l’oncle | uncle | la tante | aunt |
| le mari | husband | la femme | wife; woman |
| le cousin | cousin (m.) | la cousine | cousin (f.) |

*Fils* is pronounced "feess" — the *l* is silent. *Les parents* means both "parents" and "relatives".

## 2. The verb AVOIR — "to have"

| | AVOIR |
|---|---|
| j’ | **ai** |
| tu | **as** |
| il / elle / on | **a** |
| nous | **avons** |
| vous | **avez** |
| ils / elles | **ont** |

Don’t confuse **ils ont** ["eel-ZON", they have] with **ils sont** ["eel-SON", they are]! French uses *avoir* for age: **Ivan a vingt ans.**

Negative: **je n’ai pas de** frère — I don’t have a brother. After *pas*, the article *un, une, des* becomes **de (d’)**.

## 3. There is / there are — IL Y A

- **Il y a** un chat. — There’s a cat.
- **Il y a** beaucoup de livres. — There are lots of books.
- **Il n’y a pas de** chat. — There’s no cat.

*Il y a* is the same for singular and plural.

## 4. Possessives: my, your…

In French the possessive agrees with the **thing owned**, not the owner:

| Whose? | masc. | fem. | plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| my | **mon** père | **ma** mère | **mes** parents |
| your | **ton** frère | **ta** sœur | **tes** amis |
| his / her | **son** fils | **sa** fille | **ses** enfants |
| our | **notre** chat | **notre** maison | **nos** livres |
| your (pl., formal) | **votre** | **votre** | **vos** |
| their | **leur** | **leur** | **leurs** |

- **Son / sa** mean both "his" and "her": *Oxana et **son** frère* — Oxana and her brother (*frère* is masculine).
- Before a vowel, feminine *ma, ta, sa* become **mon, ton, son**: ***mon** amie Oxana* — my friend Oxana.

## 5. Adjectives

| m. sing. | f. sing. | m. pl. | f. pl. | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| grand | grande | grands | grandes | big, tall |
| petit | petite | petits | petites | small, little |
| brun | brune | bruns | brunes | dark-haired |
| gentil | gentille | gentils | gentilles | kind, nice |
| jeune | jeune | jeunes | jeunes | young |
| beau | belle | beaux | belles | beautiful |

Usually feminine = **+e**, plural = **+s** (silent). Most adjectives come **after** the noun (*une femme brune*), but some short common ones come **before**: *un **petit** appartement, une **belle** ville, un **grand** frère, une **jeune** fille*.

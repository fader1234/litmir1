## Meet Sasha and Dima

Sasha is a tired office worker from the city of Sinea, the heroine of the novel «La voix». One day, in the park where the dolphins disappeared, she meets a young man called Dima.

Hover over a word (or tap it) to see its translation.

> — **Bonjour**{Hello} ! **Je m’appelle**{My name is} Dima. **Et toi, comment tu t’appelles ?**{And what’s your name?}
> — **Moi, c’est**{I’m} Sasha. **Enchantée**{Nice to meet you (said by a woman)}.
> — **Enchanté**{Nice to meet you (said by a man)}. **Tu es d’où ?**{Where are you from?}
> — **Je suis de**{I’m from} Sinea. **Je suis employée de bureau**{I’m an office worker}. **Et toi ?**{And you?}
> — **Moi aussi**{Me too}, je suis de Sinea. **Ça va ?**{How are you?}
> — **Ça va bien**{I’m fine}, **merci**{thank you}. **Au revoir**{Goodbye}, Dima !
> — **Salut**{Bye}, Sasha ! **À bientôt**{See you soon} !

## 2. Introducing yourself

- **Comment tu t’appelles ?** — What’s your name? (informal)
- **Comment vous appelez-vous ?** — What’s your name? (formal)
- **Je m’appelle Sasha. / Moi, c’est Sasha.** — My name is Sasha. / I’m Sasha.
- **Enchanté / Enchantée** — Nice to meet you (man / woman)
- **Ça va ? — Ça va bien.** — How are you? — Fine.

**tu** — informal "you", **vous** — formal "you" and plural "you". In France, use *vous* with adults you don’t know.

## 3. Subject pronouns

| Français | English |
|---|---|
| je (j’) | I |
| tu | you (informal) |
| il / elle | he / she |
| on | we (informal), one |
| nous | we |
| vous | you (plural, formal) |
| ils / elles | they (masc. / fem.) |

As in English, French **never drops** the pronoun: *Je suis Sasha*. Before a vowel, *je* becomes **j’**: *j’habite* (I live).

## 4. The verb “être”

| Person | ÊTRE | Example |
|---|---|---|
| je | **suis** | Je suis Sasha. |
| tu | **es** | Tu es d’où ? |
| il / elle / on | **est** | Oxana est mon amie. |
| nous | **sommes** | Nous sommes amis. |
| vous | **êtes** | Vous êtes de Sinea ? |
| ils / elles | **sont** | Sasha et Oxana sont amies. |

No article before a profession: **Je suis employée.** — I’m an office worker. The feminine usually adds **-e**: *employé → employée*, *ami → amie* — often you can’t hear the difference!

## 5. Where are you from?

- **Tu es d’où ? / Vous êtes d’où ?** — Where are you from?
- **Je suis de Moscou / de Paris.** — I’m from Moscow / from Paris.
- **J’habite à Sinea.** — I live in Sinea.

Nationalities are written in lower case and change for gender: usually **+e**, sometimes more.

## 6. Pronunciation

| oi | "wa" | au revoir, moi |

| u | like German ü | salut, tu |

## 7. Good to know

In French there’s a space before **? ! : ;** — *Ça va ?* The stress always falls on the last syllable: *bon**jour**, enchan**té***.
